Changes made for Anatomy Finder:
- Selected skin, muscle organs, bone organs and 15 major organ structures.
- Retained individual bones/muscles as independently selectable meshes.
- Combined component meshes only within a single named anatomical structure.
- Applied one shared rigid axis rotation and uniform body scale.
- Reduced mesh density with meshoptimizer 0.22 (relative error parameter .005).
- Applied illustrative colors and exported local GLB assets.
- Added compound selection groups for skull, spine, rib cage, pelvis and muscles.

Names, FMA identifiers and parent relationships come from the official tables.
These are sourced anatomical models, not a medically validated product or a
patient-specific scan. Colors, inspection scale, arrangement and materials are
visualization choices. Function descriptions have not yet been clinically reviewed.

Reproduction scripts: scripts/import_bodyparts3d.py and scripts/optimize-atlas.mjs.
The accompanying manifest.json records structure mappings and source SHA-256 hashes.
